How To Play

AIMon is a P2P battle game where you craft AI fighters and battle other players in real-time.

1. Craft Your AIMon

Write a system prompt that defines your AIMon's personality. Keywords in your prompt determine its type, moves, and strengths:

  • FIRE — attack, burn, fire, destroy, rage, aggression
  • ICE — freeze, ice, cold, slow, chill, calm
  • TOXIC — poison, toxic, hack, corrupt, virus, drain
  • VOID — void, shadow, dark, chaos, quantum, dimension
  • SPARK — lightning, electric, speed, flash, shock, spark

2. Tune Parameters

AI parameters map directly to battle stats:

  • Temperature → CHAOS — crit chance and unpredictability
  • Top-P → ADAPT — dodge chance and versatility
  • Freq Penalty → FOCUS — accuracy and consistency
  • Max Tokens → POWER — HP and raw damage

3. Battle

Turns are simultaneous — both players pick a move, then they resolve together. Higher speed goes first. Reduce your opponent's HP to 0 to win!

4. Type Advantages

FIRE beats ICE, ICE beats SPARK, SPARK beats TOXIC, TOXIC beats VOID, VOID beats FIRE. Matching types deal 1.5x damage!
