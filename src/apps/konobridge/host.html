📖 Instructions

  1. Enter your Ignition gateway host and port (usually localhost:8088)
  2. Click Test Ignition to verify the gateway is reachable
  3. Optionally add a Tailscale API key for VPN routing
  4. Click Start Bridge to begin tunneling
  5. Share your Bridge ID with remote clients
  6. Clients open the KonoBridge viewer and enter your Bridge ID
  7. They can now access your Perspective projects through the tunnel!
